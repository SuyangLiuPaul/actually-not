Yahweh Word office tower (personal use).
3D scene code adapted from Claw3D (https://github.com/iamlukethedev/claw3d), MIT License, see LICENSE-claw3d.txt:
retro-office furniture/machine/kitchen/agent models and the furniture GLBs it ships (Kenney Furniture Kit style assets).
Project logos are the owner's own app icons.
Post-processing: postprocessing (Zlib licence, github.com/pmndrs/postprocessing) and N8AO (ISC licence, github.com/N8python/n8ao).
Also bundled: three.js, @react-three/fiber, @react-three/drei, React (all MIT).
Animated people: officelapse character layer (MIT, github.com/deepak982/officelapse, see LICENSE-officelapse.txt) with Quaternius's CC0 rig and clips via ActionForge (office-assets/people/CREDITS.md).
Contractor towers: KayKit City Builder Bits by Kay Lousberg (CC0), as bundled in Agentshire (MIT).
Plaza visitors: Paul's own 雅伟之界 models (traveller-v1, shepherd-v1).

Street traffic: cars from Kenney Car Kit by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. Tram: "Tram" by Poly by Google, CC-BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via Poly Pizza https://poly.pizza/m/e9pQgEqPGir, scaled and re-oriented.

v11 street life and wall art
- Visiting farm / zoo / parade animals (office-assets/fauna/animal-*.glb, Textures/colormap.png): Kenney "Cube Pets" (kenney.nl), CC0, via Paul's own 雅伟之界 (yahwehs-globe) project.
- Voxel giraffe and elephant (office-assets/fauna/giraffe.glb, elephant.glb): from Paul's own 雅伟之界 Ark set.
- Framed pictures (office-assets/art/*.webp): Paul's own 雅伟之界 voxel Bible scenes (no-text-derivatives-v2), cropped and resized.
- Verse text on the plaques: Chinese from Paul's edited CUV (cuvs-yhwh), English from the ASV (public domain).
- All passers-by, dogs, ducks, koi, birds, flags and props are generated in code (no third-party assets).

v12 additions
- Pocket-farm animals (office-assets/fauna/sheep.glb, goat.glb, ass.glb, dove.glb): Paul's own 雅伟之界 (yahwehs-globe) models. The cow is the Kenney "Cube Pets" cow already listed above.
- Project logos brand/sparkers.png and brand/sparklive.png: Paul's own app icons (SparkTranscribe).
- Paul's cars are the Kenney Car Kit SUV (CC0) with its colours re-baked in code. The carport, kiosk, flags, video wall and all other v12 objects are generated in code.

v14 Noah's ark (building/Ark.tsx, ArkLife.tsx)
- The 1:1 ark (135 x 22.5 x 13.5 m: gopher-wood strakes, side door, gangway, clerestory, roof) is generated in code, ported from Paul's own 雅伟之界 (yahwehs-globe, src/scene-assembly/noahs-ark-world.ts).
- Noah, his wife, Shem's and Japheth's figures, and the giraffe, elephant and camel pairs (office-assets/ark/*.glb): Paul's own 雅伟之界 models (public/models/bible-cutouts), textures cut to 256 px and meshes simplified with glTF-Transform (MIT).
- The plaque, lamps, forecourt and glow are generated in code (no third-party assets).

v14b additions (office-assets/ark/*.glb): more of Paul's own 雅伟之界 models (public/models/bible-cutouts): Ham and the three daughters-in-law, Noah in his guiding and praying poses, lion and lioness, deer, two horses, ox, lamb, squirrel, raven, dove, barrel, crate and hay bale. Textures cut to 256 px (128 px for props), meshes simplified, quantised and unused clips stripped with glTF-Transform (MIT). Nothing was downloaded from third parties in v14b.

v14e (office-assets/ark/man.glb, woman.glb, priest.glb): Paul's own 雅伟之界 models (public/models), slimmed with glTF-Transform (MIT). The traveller and shepherd are the existing office-assets/visitors models (also Paul's own).
